Frequently asked questions

What is an apartment model with a unit finder?

It is an interactive 3D model of a building or an estate embedded on the developer's website. Buyers filter available apartments by size, floor, number of rooms and price, then view the selected unit on the model.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the number of buildings and units and the level of customisation. We prepare a free quote within 24 hours of receiving basic details about your development — just fill in the form.

How long does implementation take?

Usually 10 to 15 working days, depending on the number of buildings and how complete the documentation provided by the developer is.

Does the widget work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The model is fully responsive and works on mobile devices, desktops and touch screens in the sales office.

Can I update apartment availability myself?

Yes. You update unit statuses (available, reserved, sold) in a shared Excel or Google spreadsheet, or the model pulls them directly from your CRM. The change appears on the widget automatically, with no changes to your website's code.

Can the model connect to our CRM?

Yes. If your sales system or CRM provides a list of units (via an API or an XML/CSV export), the model pulls statuses and prices from it automatically. Integrations are quoted individually.

Does the model replace the sales team?

No. It supports sales by letting buyers narrow down their choice before getting in touch, so sales conversations are shorter and more focused.

Can I embed the model on several pages?

Yes. The same widget can be placed on the development's home page, individual building pages and in marketing materials at no extra implementation cost.
